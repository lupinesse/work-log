# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project is a welcoming space for everyone. Contributors are expected to act with respect and good faith in all interactions — in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use clear, constructive language in feedback
- Accept differing opinions and approaches graciously
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not on people

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Personal attacks or derogatory comments
- Sustained disruptive behaviour in discussions
- Any form of intimidation or unwelcome contact

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that conflicts with this policy, please contact the maintainer at lupinesse@gmail.com. Reports will be handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who do not meet these standards.

---

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including the issue tracker and pull requests.
